Who Holds the Sharp Tools

Prompt Daily, Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The news is from Tuesday, October 6. Maya and Ravi are A.I. voices. Prompt Daily is produced by LumeGrid.

Mistral Large 4

Public preview on Tuesday. Weights are not out. Mistral says they land by the end of the month; an earlier Reuters excerpt gives October 27. Speech says roughly a trillion parameters and does not pick an active-parameter count, because the launch post and the current model docs disagree. Trained, Mistral says, on 3,800 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own European data centers. The preview API is live. Pricing is left out because the launch card and the current docs do not show one rate.

The cyber scores are Mistral’s claims: 82 percent on a reproduce-and-patch test, 93 percent on Cybench, and a top-five place on Artificial Analysis’s Cyber Index. Mistral also says Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra score near zero on a cyber test because they refuse. That is not an independent finding. Reduced moderation is hosted preview access for cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities. It is not a weight download.

Anthropic

Three cyber-access tiers on Tuesday. The company says public Claude still blocks most of that work. Defense is for teams protecting systems they own. Red Team is organizations only, and Anthropic says it still blocks actions such as deploying ransomware. Specialized has the fewest blocks, reviewed with the U.S. government.

On Anthropic’s own test of Opus 5.5, no access stopped every task on the first prompt, and Defense blocked 46 of 50. The company says the Red Team tier had no blocks and roughly the same completion rate as with safeguards disabled. Glasswing’s 129,000 verified vulnerabilities, April through July, is a company figure Anthropic calls an undercount. Applications are open if this is your job.

Seoul

At Tuesday’s cabinet meeting, Reuters reported President Lee pointing to signs of A.I. in some hacks, public worry, and a push to move fast and limit damage. Later reporting is separate. The Korea Herald’s tally is about 66,000 people and 2,200 corporate records across seven firms, including Shinhan’s 25,729. The Wall Street Journal, citing officials, says 68,000 people and traces of a tool called Artex. Those totals are not added together. Kim Seung-joo said the attacks may have involved Artex. That is a tentative tool name, not a confirmed attribution. The regulator shared 28 IP addresses and said an address does not show where a person sat. Tomorrow, those South Korean financial firms’ internal checks are due.

OpenAI mathematics

An unreleased internal model. The public repository lists 722 manuscripts in 372 families, from about 4,000 problems. OpenAI’s average is about three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute per result: a compute-equivalent estimate, not a measured clock time. Some proofs are in Lean. The repository says verification is incomplete and unformalized results may contain issues. The Verge says the advisory group calls the set solutions to hundreds of open questions. Before the upload, Bryna Kra at Northwestern told WIRED the request for papers mathematicians could absorb was ignored. That is one person. The papers were not read for this episode.

Smaller ships

EmbeddingGemma 2: Apache 2.0, 740 million parameters, on device. Google’s code-search score and the 20 million downloads of version one are company figures. Weights are public.

Decisions API: public beta, GPT-6 Luna only. Probabilities over predicates, fixed choices, and scores. Ten cents per million input tokens, no output charge. OpenAI’s speed comparison, as used here, is up to ten times versus GPT-6 Luna through Responses. Simon Willison is one developer who priced it against Jev.

Claude Code 2.1.292: marketplace installs, an effort setting on a sub-agent, and more sandbox and permission fixes, including a network-path read that could skip the prompt. Not retested.

Bloomberg, citing people familiar, says DeepSeek is close to at least 80 billion yuan, about 12 billion dollars, with Tencent and CATL among the largest checks, ahead of an IPO the story places in early 2027. The company did not confirm it.

Tibo, day two of the logged 28-day pledge: an earlier indexed post described Auto-review as free with a ChatGPT login and as not drawing plan usage. A second model checking the first is still not the user checking the command. Original posts were not directly retrieved.

Still ahead

Microsoft’s Windows and Surface event is later today, 11 a.m. Mountain, 10 a.m. Pacific. Mistral’s weights are still unreleased. New York is still waiting on written answers.
